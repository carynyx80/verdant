# Studio visual direction

Selected by the user: clay, coral, plum, and expressive 3D visuals.

## Implementation
- Replaced the carbon/lime system with warm cream and clay surfaces, plum text and actions, and coral accents.
- Introduced DM Sans, large editorial headlines and Instrument Serif accents.
- Rebuilt the homepage hero around a generated ceramic sculpture with floating utility and brand notes.
- Redesigned strategy cards as sculptural material studies, the footer CTA as a plum editorial panel, and launch allocation as a plum strategy canvas.
- Restyled all forms, market tables, trading panels, documentation, agent templates and wallet dialogs.
- Kept undeployed states and fund-handling disclosures visible.
